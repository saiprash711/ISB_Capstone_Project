# Operational & Business Modeling Assumptions

## 1. Inventory Policy Assumptions

All inventory policies generated by the **Inventory Engine** (`src/inventory/inventory_engine.py`) rely on explicit, configurable business parameters defined in `config/business_config.yaml`:

| Parameter | Default Value | Config Key | Rationale / Industry Benchmark |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Replenishment Lead Time ($L$)** | 2 Weeks | `inventory.lead_time_weeks` | Typical manufacturing-to-regional-depot fulfillment cycle for South Region hubs (BLR, MAA, COK, SBD, SBD1). |
| **Target Service Level ($\alpha$)** | 95.0% ($Z = 1.645$) | `inventory.target_service_level` | APICS standard for consumer durables / HVAC split AC units. |
| **Annual Holding Cost Rate** | 15.0% / year | `inventory.holding_cost_rate` | Reflects cost of warehouse storage, insurance, capital interest, and seasonal obsolescence. |
| **Unit AC Cost** | ₹25,000 INR | UI configurable | Indicative average wholesale unit cost for inverter room air conditioners (RAC). |
| **Demand Variability Method** | Lead time variance expansion ($\sigma_{LTD} = \sqrt{L} \times \sigma_{\text{forecast error}}$) | `inventory.safety_stock_method` | Assumes independent, identically distributed weekly forecast errors during replenishment lead time. |

## 2. Statistical & Data Assumptions

1. **Temporal Aggregation Grain**:
   - Invoices are aggregated to ISO Monday calendar weeks (`W-MON`). In-week daily fluctuations are considered operational delivery variations and smoothed at the weekly planning echelon.
2. **Returns & Negative Demands**:
   - Return entries, billing credit memos, and transaction reversals (negative quantities) are removed during the cleaning phase (`DataCleaner`) to prevent artificial demand deflation.
3. **External Weather Drivers**:
   - Direct ambient temperature data is not present in raw SAP billing files. The annual cooling cycle is captured by the pooled seasonal profile (region/branch month index × month-end factor, `src/forecasting/seasonal_profile.py`), estimated from past billing only. Weather shocks are not forecast. The `ScenarioSimulator` multipliers (e.g. heatwave +30%) are planning assumptions, not estimates from data.
4. **Synthetic Evaluation SKUs**:
   - The 10 `EVAL-*` SKUs (`Data_Sheet = Evaluation_10_SKUs`, generated by `generate_evaluation_data.py`) are synthetic test series. They are excluded from every page, KPI and model input unless the dashboard toggle *Include synthetic evaluation SKUs* is switched on.
5. **Indoor-Unit (IDU) Coverage**:
   - The extract carries IDU billing for Apr–Jun 2024 only. Pooled statistics and aggregate forecasts use ODU quantities, so that quarter is not double counted.
