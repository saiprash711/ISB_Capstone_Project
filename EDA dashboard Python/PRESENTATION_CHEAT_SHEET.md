## ❓ Q&A Preparation

### **"Why aren't ML models scored yet?"**
✅ "We prioritized statistical models for production first. ARIMA is running live on 42 series. ML models are built and in validation — we're being rigorous about feature engineering before scaling."

### **"What about the 28.77% data gap?"**
✅ "We've documented this as a structural issue in Q1 FY24-25. Our models train on available history. The dashboard has built-in validation to flag future gaps. Closing this gap is in our next-steps roadmap."

### **"How do you handle lumpy demand?"**
✅ "We classified SKUs using Syntetos-Boylan before model selection. 70% are lumpy, which ruled out standard exponential smoothing. That's why we built both statistical and ML tracks — let the data decide which performs better per SKU."

### **"Is this production-ready?"**
✅ "The dashboard is deployable today. ARIMA forecasts are export-ready for 42 series. Next phase is scaling SKU coverage and integrating with planning tools. We've built with production in mind — error handling, caching, modular architecture."

## ✅ Body Language & Delivery

### **Do:**
- ✅ Make eye contact with faculty/reviewers
- ✅ Use hand gestures for emphasis
- ✅ Pause after key numbers (42 series, 98.5%, 70% lumpy)
- ✅ Smile when showing the dashboard
- ✅ Invite questions after demo

### **Don't:**
- ❌ Read slides verbatim
- ❌ Apologize for unfinished ML models (frame as "rigorous validation")
- ❌ Rush through Slide 12 (your best slide)
- ❌ Go over 30 minutes
- ❌ Skip the demo

## 🚨 Emergency Backup Plans

### **If Demo Fails:**
1. Have screenshots in backup slides folder
2. Say: "Let me show you static screenshots instead"
3. Walk through the same 4-page flow using images

### **If Projector Fails:**
1. Have printed slides ready
2. Share screen via laptop (if possible)
3. Offer to email presentation afterward

### **If Running Late:**
- Skip Slide 14 (Technical Architecture)
- Keep demo to 2 minutes (just forecasting page)
- Maintain Slide 12 (Challenges) — never cut this
